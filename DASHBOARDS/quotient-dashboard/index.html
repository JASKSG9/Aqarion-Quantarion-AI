BOUNDARY CONDITIONS

Interpretation notes

  • Every subgroup of an abelian group is normal, so every subgroup H≤G defines a quotient group G/H.
  • The quotient is finite exactly when its Smith-normal-form decomposition has free rank zero.
  • A quotient of a cyclic group is cyclic. A quotient of a non-cyclic finitely generated abelian group may be cyclic or non-cyclic.
  • The quotient free rank cannot exceed the ambient free rank.
  • Torsion-free ambient groups can have torsion quotients, for example ℤ/2ℤ is a quotient of ℤ.
  • A single translation action only produces global coset closure when its translation generators span the ambient group. This dashboard computes algebraic quotients, not action-orbit closure by itself.
