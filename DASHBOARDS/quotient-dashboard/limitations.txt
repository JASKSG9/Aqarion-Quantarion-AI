Supported:
- Finite abelian groups.
- Finitely generated abelian groups.
- Finite lists of subgroup generators.
- Exact integer input.

Not supported:
- Arbitrary infinitely generated abelian groups.
- Nonabelian quotients.
- Automatic subgroup-membership proof from non-coordinate input.
- Formal proof-assistant certification.
- Action-orbit closure under one non-generating translation.
