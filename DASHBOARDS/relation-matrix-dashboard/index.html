SCOPE NOTES

Interpretation boundaries

  • The dashboard computes the cokernel (operatorname{coker}(A)=mathbb Z^m/operatorname{im}(A)).
  • If (A) has (m) rows, the quotient has free rank (m-operatorname{rank}(A)).
  • The quotient has finite order exactly when its free rank is zero.
  • If finite, its order is the product of its nontrivial invariant factors.
  • The quotient is cyclic when it has at most one nontrivial torsion factor and no free rank greater than one.
  • This is an exact finite-integer computation, but it is not a Lean, Coq, or other proof-assistant formalization.
